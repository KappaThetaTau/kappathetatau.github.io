Who we are

Theta Tau is

the oldest, largest, and foremost Fraternity for Engineers. Founded at the University of Minnesota in 1904, it has initiated over 40,000 members. The Fraternity emphasizes quality and a strong fraternal bond, with chapters only at ABET accredited schools and limited chapter sizes. Read more about our history here!


Kappa has over 90 active members and continues to grow. Each semester, the chapter hosts professional, philanthropic, and social events, attends leadership conferences and the Engineering Open House, and volunteers through campus engineering programs. Members also enjoy social events like dinners, camping, and pick-up sports, fueling Kappa's rapid growth in recent years.
